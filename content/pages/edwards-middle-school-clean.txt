## Mission Statement
R.C. Edwards Middle School, with the student, family, and community, will promote high standards of academic achievement, personal achievement, and career preparation, in a safe environment.
## Vision Statement
We envision a school where all students will be prepared to be productive citizens in an ever-changing technology-driven world.
