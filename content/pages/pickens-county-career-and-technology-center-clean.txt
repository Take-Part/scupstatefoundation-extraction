## Mission Statement
The mission is to provide students with opportunities to explore their talents and interests, matching them with career opportunities and fostering the skills needed for success in college and the competitive workforce. PCCTC works to build partnerships with industry, post-secondary schools, and high schools to create these valuable educational experiences for its students.
