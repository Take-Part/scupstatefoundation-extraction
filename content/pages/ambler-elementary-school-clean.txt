## Mission Statement
Ambler Elementary School, committed to academic excellence, will provide a strong foundation for all students through a safe, nurturing environment where staff, family, and community work together to emphasize quality technology-rich educational opportunities that encourage success.
## Vision Statement
Ambler Elementary School will strive to provide a quality 21st century education that engages all students for success.
