## About Us
## Mission Statement
We invest in innovative education and community development to uplift children and families in Pickens County South Carolina.
## Vision Statement
To build a future where families thrive in a safe, vibrant community that values virtuous character, wellness, and education, and where robust careers and economic development create lasting prosperity for generations to come.

## Danny Youngblood
Danny is a Pickens County native and a 1977 Graduate of Clemson University. ## Pastor John Adams
John Adams is the Pastor of First Baptist Church, Easley since 2001. He has served churches in North Carolina, Virginia, and Texas. He married the love of his life, Donna, in 1983. They are immensely and incredibly blessed with a son, Joshua and his wife, Alexa.

John Adams is a (mostly) retired advertising executive. ## Dr. Danny Merck
connect@scupstatefoundation.org

## Dr. Danny Merck
Dr. Danny Merck is in his 34th year as an educator in South Carolina and has served as Superintendent of the School District of Pickens County (SDPC) for 11 years. Under his leadership, SDPC has achieved historic milestones, including record-high graduation rates, competitive teacher pay, the elimination of teacher shortages, and nationally recognized academic and career readiness programs.
A champion of leadership through service, Dr. Merck fosters a Winsome Culture built on trust, joy, and continuous improvement. His inverted pyramid leadership model ensures that all 2,100 SDPC employees are supported in their mission to serve over 16,000 students. He has established leadership development programs such as SDPC’s Leadership Academy to prepare future school and district leaders.
Following a 2018 education leadership trip to Finland, Dr. Merck cultivated a global partnership with the University of Oulu, bringing innovative teacher training, leadership development, and instructional best practices to SDPC. He has also been instrumental in workforce development, collaborating with local industries to provide students with career training, internships, and certifications.
Dr. Merck has served on the South Carolina Education Oversight Committee, engaged in national superintendent dialogues, and led SDPC’s advancements in technology, including a countywide fiber network and a top-tier virtual learning infrastructure. His leadership ensured one of the highest in person instruction rates in the nation during the COVID-19 pandemic.
He holds a B.A. in Business Economics from Wofford College, an Ed.D. in Educational Leadership from Nova Southeastern University, and a Superintendent Certification from Clemson University. He and his wife, Suzanne, live in Norris, SC, and have four children: a teacher, a nurse, a University of Oklahoma football player, and a Presbyterian College soccer player.
With a passion for servant leadership and student success, Dr. Merck continues to shape the future of education in Pickens County and beyond.

## Dr. Brian Swords
connect@scupstatefoundation.org

## Dr. Brian Swords
Dr. Swords, a lifelong Pickens County resident and Liberty High School graduate, is the President and CEO of the Easley Chamber of Commerce. He spent over 32 years in higher education, most recently as Vice President for Enrollment Management and Interim Vice President of Operations at Southern Wesleyan University, where he led restructuring efforts that saved over $1 million annually, increased student retention, and launched the university’s first early college program and a dual-degree partnership with Clemson University.
Before retiring from Tri-County Technical College after 29 years, Swords served as Executive Director of Campuses, Training Centers, and Online Programs. He expanded the college’s reach by establishing satellite campuses in Anderson, Oconee, and Pickens counties and led the development of an Enterprise Campus to support economic growth.
In addition to leading the Easley Chamber, Swords is a Professor of Education and Business in Southern Wesleyan University’s Graduate School and has taught at Clemson and Anderson universities. He remains active in the Community Colleges of Appalachia Association and serves on numerous boards, including Leadership South Carolina, Leadership Pickens County, and the National Association of Computer Forensics Examiners. He previously served as chairman of the Easley Chamber and was elected to three terms on the Pickens County School Board.
Dr. Swords holds degrees from Southern Wesleyan and Clemson University, including a doctorate in Higher Education Leadership. He completed post-doctoral work at the University of North Carolina-Chapel Hill’s School of Government.
He resides in Liberty with his two adopted sons and is a member of Newspring Church in Powdersville.
