## Mission Statement
Our mission is to engage and empower every student to SOAR by creating a supportive environment where all learners are valued, challenged, and celebrated for who they are and what they can become. Through shared commitment and a culture of excellence, we achieve success together.
## Vision Statement
At Hagood Elementary, we engage students today to empower them for success tomorrow—together achieving excellence in learning, leadership, and life.
