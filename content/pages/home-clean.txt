We invest in innovative education and community development to uplift children and families in Pickens County South Carolina.

## Our Mission in Action
We exist to bridge the gap between what schools need and what public funding provides. Our mission is to ensure that every child—regardless of background—has the opportunity to learn, thrive, and achieve their fullest potential.
We are an authentically American school district—rooted in local values, built by hard-working families, and guided by a deep respect for our neighbors’ desire for low taxes. This commitment means that public funding alone cannot meet every need of our students and teachers.
That’s where we step in. We invite our neighbors, business owners, and community members to journey alongside us—investing their time, talent, and treasure to help educate the next generation of Pickens County residents.
## How You Can Make an Impact
At the Upstate Education & Opportunity Foundation, every gift—of time, talent, or treasure—creates real change for children and families across Pickens County.
## 1. Discern Your Calling
Begin by reflecting on how you feel called to give back to the world. Every person has a unique gift to offer—some through their time and service, others through their expertise, and many through their financial generosity. However you are moved to give, your contribution helps shape a stronger future for our community’s children.
## 2. Choose How You Give
Unrestricted Gifts
support the Foundation’s Greatest Needs. These funds allow us to act quickly when challenges arise—whether it’s emergency aid for a family, support for mental health initiatives, or funding for innovative classroom projects.
Restricted Gifts
allow benefactors to invest directly in specific schools or district-wide initiatives—from special education and arts programs to workforce development and experiential learning.
## 3. See Your Impact in Action
Each month, schools receive direct funding from the gifts designated for them. Benefactors see their generosity at work through updates, photos, and stories that highlight the lives changed because of their investment.
## 4. Join the Movement
Give your time by volunteering in schools, share your talent by mentoring or providing expertise, or offer your treasure by making a one-time or recurring gift. Together, we are transforming education in Pickens County—one child at a time.

## Equip Every Child to Thrive
When you give to special education, you’re helping create a world where every child—regardless of ability—can learn, grow, and thrive.
## Your gift helps fund:
• Adaptive equipment like communication devices, sensory tools, and mobility aids
• Specialized classroom materials tailored to diverse learning needs
• Inclusive programs that support social, emotional, and academic development
Every donation helps ensure students with disabilities are not left behind—but fully included, supported, and empowered.
Together, we can create learning environments where all children are seen, valued, and given the tools they need to succeed.

## Support Arts & Music Education: Let Every Child Find Their Voice
Arts and music education spark creativity, build confidence, and open doors for students to express themselves in powerful ways. From paintbrushes to banjos, these experiences help children discover who they are—and what they’re capable of.
## Your gift helps provide:
• Instruments, supplies, and performance materials for school programs
• Access to transformative programs like YAMS (Young Appalachian Musicians), which preserve regional culture while teaching discipline and joy through music
• Creative spaces where students can paint, dance, act, and sing their way to deeper learning
When we invest in the arts, we invest in the whole child—mind, heart, and spirit.
Help us keep the arts alive in every school. Because every child deserves a chance to be heard.

## Transform Classrooms into Spaces that Inspire
The right environment can spark creativity, focus, and collaboration. Innovative classroom furniture goes beyond desks and chairs—it invites movement, interaction, and flexible thinking.
## Your gift helps provide:
• Flexible seating options like wobble stools, standing desks, and soft seating areas
• Collaborative furniture that allows students to work in teams, problem-solve, and share ideas
• Interactive tools like mobile whiteboards and tech-integrated tables that make learning dynamic
These tools are more than comfort—they’re catalysts for engagement, equity, and empowerment.
Help us design learning spaces where students don’t just sit—they think, explore, and thrive.

## Support Student Wellness: Healthy Minds, Healthy Futures
Children can’t learn if they’re overwhelmed, anxious, or unwell. That’s why investing in mental health and physical wellness is essential to ensuring every child has the foundation they need to succeed—inside and outside the classroom.
## Your gift helps provide:
• Housing support for families experiencing homelessness
• Wellness spaces and calming rooms that give students a safe place to reset and refocus
• Physical wellness programs, including fitness equipment, hydration stations, movement-based learning tools, and healthy nutrition education
• Training for teachers and staff in trauma-informed care and student mental health first aid
From mindfulness moments to movement breaks, we are creating schools where children feel safe, strong, and supported—every single day.
Support whole-child wellness—and help every student grow with confidence, strength, and hope.
make a gift
## Invest in Athletics: Build Character, Teamwork, and Opportunity
School athletics do more than build strong bodies—they build strong leaders. Through sports, students learn discipline, teamwork, resilience, and pride in themselves and their schools.
## Your gift helps provide:
• Updated equipment and safe uniforms for student-athletes across the district
• Facility upgrades and field improvements that benefit both students and the community
• Equitable access to sports programs for all students, regardless of income
• Character-building coaching and leadership programs that extend far beyond the field
Athletics help students stay engaged in school, build lifelong friendships, and develop the confidence to tackle challenges on and off the field.
When you support school athletics, you’re investing in more than a game—you’re investing in a generation.
