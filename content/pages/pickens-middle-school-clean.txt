## Mission Statement
To prepare students for college and career readiness, Pickens Middle School, in partnership with parents, students, businesses, and the community, emphasizes rigorous standards in language arts, math, science, technology, engineering, fine arts, and social sciences through diverse learning opportunities in a secure and caring environment.
## Vision Statement
Empowering students to realize the best in themselves and in others.
