## Mission Statement
Pickens High School strives to reach all students in a caring environment that supplies students with rigorous instruction, inspiring each to be of great character, lifelong learners, and leaders in the community and beyond.
## Vision Statement
Ignite the Blue Flame of excellence in every student by providing a safe, caring, and rigorous academic environment through providing opportunities that enhance student achievement and promote student engagement.
